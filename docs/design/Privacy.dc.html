Privacy

Last updated 20 August 2026

What we store

Your email address and an account id from our sign-in provider, the plan you are on, and a count of relists you have made. That is the whole list.

What we never see

Your Marktplaats password, your Marktplaats session, and the contents of your ads. Relisting runs entirely in your own browser under your own Marktplaats login — the ad text and photos are copied from Marktplaats to Marktplaats and never reach us. We only ever receive the id of an ad you relisted.

Payments

Card details go straight to Stripe and are never handled by AdBump. We store the customer reference Stripe gives us so we know which plan you are on.

Who else gets it

Nobody. We do not sell data, and there is no analytics or advertising code in the extension.

Deleting your account

Email us and everything above is removed. Your Marktplaats ads are unaffected — they were never ours.
