Tokens

The palette was re-cut in 2026-08 for the surfaces that now exist: a cooler neutral ramp, a stronger accent, and a dark theme that is its own design rather than the light one inverted. Three tokens carry the modern chrome — --subtle (the fill under a table head or an input), --ring (the focus ring, which nothing had) and --shadow-sm (a resting card, where --shadow is a lifted one). This is what T28 encodes.
