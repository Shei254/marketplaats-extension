What Bumpr does

Bumpr deletes one of your Marktplaats ads and immediately posts it again, so it reappears at the top of recent listings. That is the whole product.

This is destructive, and you should know it

Relisting means the original ad is deleted. Bumpr saves every field and photo first and restores them, but if Marktplaats rejects the new ad the old one is already gone. Do not relist an ad you cannot afford to lose.

Your Marktplaats account is yours

You are responsible for how you use your own account, including Marktplaats’ own terms. Bumpr paces relists to keep behaviour reasonable, but it cannot promise Marktplaats will not object.

Plans and quotas

Each plan grants a number of relists per month. Unused relists do not carry over. We may change what a plan grants; if we lower it, you keep what you already paid for until your period ends.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from the Stripe portal. You keep your paid plan until the period you paid for runs out, then drop back to the free plan — you are never locked out of your own ads.
